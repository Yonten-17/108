How to Play

WASD
MoveWalk around the arena.
AimMove the mouse. Your barrel follows the cursor.
Left click
ShootHold the left mouse button to keep firing.
Space
DashHold a move key, then press Space for a quick burst. Recharges in 2 seconds.
R
RestartStart a new round.
The white ring is your life: 10 hits and you’re out. Blocks stop bullets. A yellow plus gives +4 life and appears somewhere new every 20 seconds. Red bombs appear every 5 seconds. Shoot a bomb to make it explode and damage everyone nearby. Click the arena to lock your cursor inside it, and press Esc to release it. Press P to pause and M to mute.
